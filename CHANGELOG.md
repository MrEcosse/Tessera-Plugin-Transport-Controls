# Changelog

## 0.2.1
- README: the use case (a set of four wall-panel tiles for conference rooms, sports bars and the like).

## 0.2.0
- The tile can now be a single cell. When the keys do not fit side by side at a finger's width, they wrap onto two or
  three rows. Wider tiles keep one row.
- A tile placed at 1x1 can now be widened by dragging its edge, also on a screen with two columns.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile of media keys (skip back, stop, play, pause, play/pause, skip forward, eject), each running the
  Home Assistant action you give it. Keys without an action are left out.
